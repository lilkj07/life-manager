Data & Backup

Your data is saved automatically to a file on this computer. A daily backup is kept for the last 14 days. Use Export to save a copy anywhere (e.g. a USB drive or cloud folder), and Import to restore from one.

Accounts

Your banks and cash pockets. Every income and expense entry is logged against one of these; rename an account and its entries follow.

Transfers

Moving your own money between accounts — cash into the bank, one bank to another. Recorded here instead of as an expense plus an income, so your income and spending totals stay honest. Account balances update; nothing else does.
